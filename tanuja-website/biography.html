Biography

My name is Tanuja Dharana. I was born on 24 March 2005. I studied until Grade 5 at Andiambalama Buddhist Primary School. After passing the scholarship examination, I entered D.S. Senanayake College, Colombo 07 in 2016.

I currently work as a Cameraman and Main Video Editor for my mother’s YouTube channel “Super Mind with Samanthee” (Samanthee Deshapriya).

Alongside ICT teaching, I also provide professional photography services and manage my own photography portfolio.
